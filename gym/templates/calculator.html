Understanding Your Numbers

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index is a universal screening tool relating height to body weight. While useful for general population baselines, muscular lifters should also reference InBody composition scans.

What is BMR vs TDEE?

BMR is the base energy required just to keep organs alive in bed. TDEE adds your daily walking, lifting, digestion, and job activity to give your true maintenance energy.

How to calculate macros?

Set protein at 1.6 - 2.2g per kg of body weight, dietary fats at 0.7 - 1g per kg, and fill the remaining caloric allowance with complex carbs for glycogen replenishment.
