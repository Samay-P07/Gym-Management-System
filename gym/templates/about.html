THE FITCORE STORY

From A Garage Weight Rack To Premier Athletic Arena

Founded by Patel Samay, FITCORE emerged to solve a fundamental frustration with modern fitness centers: lack of biomechanically sound equipment, overcrowded free-weight areas, and generic copy-paste workout routines.

We built a 14,000 sq.ft facility engineered specifically around athlete biomechanics. From calibrated steel Olympic plates to infrared recovery saunas and automated digital membership passes, every square foot is designed to help you execute your training without friction.
